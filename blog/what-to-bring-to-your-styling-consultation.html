What to Bring to Your Styling Consultation

A great consultation isn't about arriving with all the answers. It's about arriving with enough to have a real conversation. Here's what actually helps.

Reference images, but curated, not overwhelming

Save 5–10 images that capture the feeling you're after, more than the exact garment. A folder of 80 unrelated photos makes it harder, not easier, for your stylist to find the thread connecting what you love. Quality of reference beats quantity, every time.

Budget clarity, even if it feels uncomfortable

Being upfront about your budget range isn't awkward. It's the single most useful thing you can bring. It lets your stylist recommend fabrics, detailing, and construction choices that genuinely fit your investment, rather than designing something that has to be scaled back later.

Honest answers about your day

How long will you be in the piece? Will you be standing for hours, dancing, sitting through a long ceremony? Is your venue outdoor, air-conditioned, humid? These practical details shape real decisions about fabric weight and structure, far more than most people expect.

An open mind about what you don't know yet

Some of the best results come from a stylist gently steering you away from an idea that wouldn't have suited your body, your event, or your budget, and toward something you didn't know to ask for. Come ready to listen as much as to describe.

What you don't need to bring

You don't need to have your final decision made, a fully resolved vision, or even total certainty about the occasion details. That's exactly what the consultation is for.
