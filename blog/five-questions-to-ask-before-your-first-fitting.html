Five Questions to Ask Before Your First Fitting

The Fitting.

Your first bridal consultation sets the tone for everything that follows: the silhouette you land on, the fabric you fall in love with, the timeline you build your wedding around. Walking in with the right questions in mind means you leave with clarity, not just inspiration.

1. What silhouette actually suits how I move?

Photos are a starting point, not a verdict. A silhouette that looks stunning on a runway may sit completely differently on your frame, or feel restrictive the moment you need to dance, sit, or hug your grandmother. Ask your stylist to talk you through how a silhouette will feel, not just how it will photograph.

2. What's the realistic timeline for my date?

Custom bridal wear isn't something to rush. Ask directly: given my wedding date, when does the design need to be finalized, when are fittings scheduled, and what happens if my measurements shift closer to the day? A good atelier will give you real dates, not vague reassurance.

3. What fabric is right for my venue and season?

A heavy structured satin tells a different story than a fluid silk chiffon, and the right choice depends on where and when you're saying "I do." Ask what fabric performs best for your specific setting, not just what looks best on a hanger.

4. How many fittings should I expect?

Multiple fittings aren't a sign something went wrong in the first pass. They're how precision happens. Ask how many are typical for a piece like yours, so you can plan your calendar and your patience accordingly.

5. What's included, and what isn't?

Alterations after the wedding, preservation, travel for fittings: get specific about what's part of your package and what's an added cost, before you fall in love with anything.

Walking in prepared doesn't make the process less magical. It makes it yours. Bring your questions, your references, and an open mind, and let the rest be built around you.
