Inside Our Quality Control Process

Behind every finished piece that leaves our atelier is a process most clients never see: daily supervision, order tracking, and a level of workflow discipline that exists for one reason, so nothing reaches you that isn't exactly right.

Daily supervision, not spot-checks

Production isn't something we check in on occasionally. It's actively supervised every day. Every order in progress is reviewed daily against its specifications, so issues are caught at the stage they happen, not discovered at a final fitting when there's far less room to fix them.

Order tracking, from fabric to finish

Every piece is tracked through each stage of construction (cutting, assembly, detailing, pressing), so we always know exactly where an order stands, and so nothing gets lost in the handoff between stages, even when multiple orders are moving through the atelier at once.

Workflow discipline as a quality tool

Consistency doesn't happen by accident. Clear workflows, defined checkpoints, and disciplined handoffs between our production team mean the same standard applies whether it's one order in progress or several. This is what allows us to manage multiple bridal, couture, and ready-to-wear orders simultaneously without any single one losing the attention it deserves.

Why this matters to you

You're not just paying for a finished garment. You're paying for the process that guarantees it arrives the way it was promised, on the timeline you were given. Quality control isn't a final inspection bolted onto the end of production; it's built into every day of it.

It's not the most visible part of what we do, but it's often the part that matters most.
