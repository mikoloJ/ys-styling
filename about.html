The House

Craft is our language.

YS Styling was founded on a simple belief: that clothing built entirely around one person, their story, their proportions, their occasion, will always outlast what's bought off a rack. We work in bridal, couture, and ready-to-wear, but the approach never changes.

Susan Idiareh - Founder

Personalized, from concept to final fitting.

Every YS Styling piece begins with a conversation, not a catalogue. We bring creative concepts to life for bridal, couture, ready-to-wear, and custom collections, overseeing production in-house so quality, cost, and timelines stay firmly within our control and, in turn, yours.

Brand values.

Craftsmanship

Every seam, silhouette, and finish is executed with a level of precision that respects the client's investment.

Exclusivity

Limited consultation slots protect the time, attention, and detail every client's piece deserves.

Personalization

No templates. Every piece is built to reflect the client's individual story and occasion.

Integrity in Delivery

Timelines and costs are communicated clearly and honoured, with no surprises at the final fitting.

Confidence

The measure of a great piece is how a client carries themselves the moment they put it on.

Quality Control

An in-house production process built on daily supervision, order tracking, and workflow discipline.
